Utility resources

Quick explanations members can use before asking for support.

01

Utility vs. supplier

Your utility delivers service and manages the local account. In competitive energy markets, a separate supplier may appear on the bill for generation or supply charges.

02

Read the amount due

Check the current charges, past-due balance, due date, and any notices before focusing only on the total.

03

When you receive a notice

Read the effective date, reason, and contact instructions. Acting early usually gives you more time to understand available options.

04

Moving or starting service

Start the process before move-in day and have identification, address details, and any requested account information available.
